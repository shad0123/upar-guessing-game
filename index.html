How to play

Two players are enough — one guesses while the others give clues. A big group is even better.

Playing a round

  1. Add players, pick deck(s) and set the timer on the setup page. Tap several decks to mix them. In Team mode (choose Team after tapping Play Offline), put each player in a team (A–D) in the Player List and pick 2, 3 or 4 teams — each team needs at least 2 players.
  2. Hold the phone to your forehead, screen facing your friends. Turn it sideways.
  3. Friends act it out or describe it — gestures, sounds and short hints. Don't say the word itself.
  4. Guess, then tilt down for a correct answer or tilt up to pass. Tap the top or bottom of the screen if you prefer.
  5. Beat the timer, then pass the phone on. In Solo mode the next player guesses next, until everyone has had a turn — highest score wins. In Team mode the next team goes, with a different guesser each time that team plays; every team gets the same number of turns and the highest team total wins.

Tips for a better game

  • Keep the pace quick. Pass fast on hard words — every second counts.
  • Use both languages. The Hinglish line under each word gives clue-givers a second way in.
  • Switch guessers every round. In Solo mode everyone guesses once; in Teams mode, a different member of the team guesses each time that team plays.
  • Shorter rounds, more rounds. 30–60 seconds keeps the room loud and laughing.
  • Kids playing? Start with the Kids deck — simple, familiar words for family game night.

Frequently asked questions

Can I mix decks together?

Yes — tap as many decks as you like and they play as one mixed round. Tap a selected deck again to remove it, use “Surprise mix” for 3 random decks, or pick the Mixed deck for everything at once. The word count updates as you tap, so you always know how big the round will be.

Is UPAR free?

Yes — completely free to play in your browser, with every deck unlocked and no download required.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It runs in any modern browser on iPhone and Android. Menus work in portrait; turn the phone sideways for the guessing round. You can install it from your browser menu (Add to Home screen / Install app) — after the first visit it keeps working offline, with no download from an app store.

How do the two languages work?

Every card shows the word in English on top and Hinglish underneath, at the same time. There is no language switch — both are always visible, so clue-givers can use whichever helps.

Can we play on separate phones?

Yes — tap “Play Online” on the home screen. One player creates a room and shares the 4-letter room code; everyone else joins with that code (any number of players, no cap). Each round one player is the Guesser — phone on their forehead, word hidden, tilting to score or pass — and everyone else sees the word on their own phone and gives clues over a call. The Guesser rotates every round, and the word, timer and score stay in sync on every screen. Online play needs internet; the offline game on this page still works with no connection at all.

Can we play in teams?

Yes. Tap Play Offline and choose Team, then pick 2, 3 or 4 teams in the round settings. Put each player in a team on the Player List window (offline) or by tapping a team on your lobby card (online) — each team needs at least 2 players, and teams don't need to be equal. Turns rotate through the teams, a different member guesses each time a team plays, every team gets the same number of turns, scores add up into each team's total, and the highest total wins — no word repeats in the same session.

How many people can play?

At least two: one guesses while the others give clues. One phone is enough for the whole room — pass it around so everyone gets a turn. It works for birthdays, house parties, classrooms, offsites and family game nights.

Is it good for kids and families?

Yes. The Kids deck has simple, familiar words, which makes it a great free game for family game night and playing with children.

Can I use my own words?

Yes — open “Choose decks”, tap the Your Words tile at the end of the grid, and add your own words in the window that opens. Add at least 4, then start a round with just your deck.
